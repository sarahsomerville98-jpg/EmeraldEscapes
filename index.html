Welcome to Emerald Escapes

At Emerald Escapes, we believe that some of the world's most unforgettable adventures are waiting right here in Ireland. From breathtaking coastal landscapes to historic castles and vibrant local traditions, we help travellers discover the beauty and character of the Emerald Isle.

Emerald Escapes was founded in Galway by a family with a shared love for Ireland's breathtaking landscapes, rich history, and unique culture. What began as a family pastime organising monthly trips became a family-run travel company in 2012. Emerald Escapes is dedicated to helping visitors discover the very best of Ireland, from its iconic landmarks to its hidden gems.

Whether you're an adventure seeker, a culture enthusiast, or simply looking for a relaxing getaway, our carefully selected experiences offer something for everyone.
